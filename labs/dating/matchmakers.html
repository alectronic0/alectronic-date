Before you sign with a matchmaker

  • Ask how many active members match your brief, in your area and age range, and get it in writing. In Burki v Seventy Thirty (2018) the High Court found an agency had misstated its membership numbers and ordered the £12,600 fee repaid. That company has since been dissolved.
  • Know your cancellation rights. Contracts signed online or away from the agency's premises usually carry a 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts Regulations.
  • Get the number of introductions guaranteed, and what happens if they don't arrive.
  • Check the refund terms before paying a large fee upfront.
  • Look for ABIA membership. Members agree to the ABIA code of practice.
